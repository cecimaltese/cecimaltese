🇮🇹 Italian, based in Munich. MSc in Automotive Engineering at **TUM**, BSc in Automation Engineering (robotics major) at **Politecnico di Milano**.

I like the part of engineering where software meets something physical: a robot that has to stay upright, a rover on fake Martian gravel, a battery container that has to report its power correctly at 4 in the morning. Most of my work sits between control, learning, and the very unglamorous integration work that makes hardware actually talk.

🔭 **Right now**

- Training an 8-DOF bipedal robot (**Dodo**) to walk and backflip in NVIDIA IsaacLab at **TUM MIRMI**, aiming at sim-to-real
- Commissioning and validating battery storage systems (Modbus TCP, EMS integration, data plausibility) at **Entrix**
- Looking for a robotics **Forschungspraxis** topic for WS 26/27, ideally locomotion, exoskeletons, or space robotics
